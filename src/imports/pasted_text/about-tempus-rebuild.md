The current "About Tempus" page no longer reflects the direction we want for the brand.

The page became too conceptual and too focused on inspirational statements.

Although the editorial language is good, the page no longer communicates the complete Tempus ecosystem.

This page should not feel like a manifesto.

It should feel like discovering a premium furniture brand.

Use the current version only as a starting point.

Rebuild the page hierarchy based on the following principles.

--------------------------------------------------
MAIN OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------

The institutional page must communicate the entire Tempus universe.

Visitors should leave understanding:

• who Tempus is

• what Tempus designs

• why Tempus is different

• the technologies behind the products

• the customization possibilities

• why architects and designers specify Tempus

The page should balance emotion, products and engineering.

--------------------------------------------------
PAGE STRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------

Reorganize the page into the following chapters.

--------------------------------------------------
1. HERO
--------------------------------------------------

Maintain a strong architectural hero image.

Large typography.

Minimal copy.

One CTA.

--------------------------------------------------
2. OUR PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------

Present the Tempus vision.

Do not speak about products yet.

Speak about:

comfort

time

architecture

well-being

permanence

Keep this section concise.

Avoid long manifestos.

--------------------------------------------------
3. OUR PRODUCTS
--------------------------------------------------

Introduce the product universe.

Display the five official product categories:

• Beds

• Recliners

• Tempus Office

• Recliner Accessories

• Sofas

Use real Tempus photography.

This section should clearly communicate what the company actually designs.

Do not mention collections.

Collections do not exist in the Tempus brand architecture.

--------------------------------------------------
4. ENGINEERING & TECHNOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------

Create a dedicated chapter presenting the four exclusive engineering systems.

Present them as one family of technologies.

Include:

• Unitech

• Duotech

• Multitech

• Fulltech

Do not create four different pages.

Do not treat them as products.

Present them as proprietary engineering systems developed by Tempus.

Give this section visual importance.

--------------------------------------------------
5. TEMPUS SIGNATURE
--------------------------------------------------

Create a dedicated editorial chapter introducing Tempus Signature.

Present it as the personalization philosophy of the brand.

Not as a product.

Not as a collection.

Use premium photography showing:

• fabrics

• leather

• stitching

• finishes

• craftsmanship

Keep the copy concise.

Allow the imagery to communicate luxury.

--------------------------------------------------
6. FOR PROFESSIONALS
--------------------------------------------------

Restore the Professionals section.

This section must not be removed.

It is strategically important.

Create a dedicated chapter for:

Architects

Interior Designers

Specification Professionals

Present Tempus as a design partner.

Include:

• editorial lifestyle photography

• short introduction

• clear CTA

Examples:

Become a Partner

Professional Area

Specification Program

This section should build credibility.

--------------------------------------------------
7. MATERIALS & CRAFTSMANSHIP
--------------------------------------------------

Present the quality behind the products.

Use close-up photography.

Leather.

Wood.

Fabric.

Stitching.

Construction.

Allow materials to become the visual focus.

--------------------------------------------------
8. CLOSING
--------------------------------------------------

Finish with a strong emotional image.

One headline.

One CTA.

Allow the page to end quietly.

--------------------------------------------------
REMOVE
--------------------------------------------------

Reduce the number of oversized conceptual headlines.

Not every section needs a poetic statement.

Avoid repeating the same editorial formula throughout the page.

Avoid creating a sequence of inspirational quotes.

Avoid making every section feel like a manifesto.

--------------------------------------------------
VISUAL RHYTHM
--------------------------------------------------

Alternate:

large photography

↓

brand information

↓

products

↓

technology

↓

professionals

↓

materials

↓

closing image

The page should constantly alternate between emotion and information.

--------------------------------------------------
PRODUCT PRESENCE
--------------------------------------------------

Increase the presence of real Tempus products.

The current page contains too many lifestyle scenes and not enough furniture.

Use authentic product photography throughout the page.

The visitor should never forget that Tempus is a premium furniture brand.

--------------------------------------------------
DESIGN LANGUAGE
--------------------------------------------------

Maintain the existing Tempus design system.

Maintain:

• typography

• spacing

• colors

• grid

• editorial style

• white as the primary canvas

Avoid introducing unnecessary colored sections.

Photography should define the rhythm—not background colors.

--------------------------------------------------
IMPORTANT
--------------------------------------------------

Do not build a corporate "About Us" page.

Do not build a pure manifesto.

Build a premium brand presentation.

Every chapter should reinforce one of the brand's pillars.

The visitor should finish the page with a clear understanding of:

• The Tempus philosophy.

• The product categories.

• The exclusive technologies.

• Tempus Signature.

• The Professionals Program.

• The craftsmanship behind the products.

The final page should feel elegant, architectural, premium and timeless while remaining informative and commercially effective.
