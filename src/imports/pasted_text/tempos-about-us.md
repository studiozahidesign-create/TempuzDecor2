Create the complete “About Us” page for Tempos, with a much more institutional and brand-focused narrative.

IMPORTANT:

The previous direction focused too heavily on products, product categories and technology.

This page must NOT feel like another product presentation.

The purpose of this page is to communicate:

• Who Tempos is
• What the company represents
• Its history
• Its way of working
• Its people
• Its structure
• Its relationships
• Its presence in the market
• Its vision for the future

The brand must be the protagonist.

Products and technology should appear only as evidence of the company's capabilities, not as the main subject.

GLOBAL DESIGN RULES

• Keep the existing Tempos design system exactly as approved.
• Never use serif typography.
• Use Clash Display and the established sans-serif typography.
• Every major section should occupy approximately 100vh on desktop.
• Maintain the existing color palette.
• Do not introduce new colors.
• No heavy shadows.
• No glassmorphism.
• No decorative graphics.
• No excessive cards.
• No generic corporate UI.
• Use large, sophisticated photography.
• Keep layouts structured, editorial and architectural.
• Avoid excessive empty space.
• Every section must have a clear narrative purpose.
• Text must remain visually aligned with the main image or visual composition.
• Use subtle motion and transitions, but avoid excessive animation.

The page should feel like the institutional presentation of a premium furniture company, not a product catalog.
