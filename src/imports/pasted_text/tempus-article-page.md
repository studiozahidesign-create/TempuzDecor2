Create the individual article page for the Tempus Journal.

The page must feel like a premium editorial publication about design, architecture, interiors and contemporary living.

It should feel connected to the Tempus website, but the article itself should have a stronger editorial and reading-focused experience.

Do not make it look like a conventional WordPress blog post.

Do not use excessive cards, sidebars, boxes or decorative UI.

GLOBAL RULES

• Keep the existing Tempus design system.
• Use Clash Display and the existing sans-serif typography.
• Never use serif fonts.
• Maintain the existing color palette.
• No gradients.
• No heavy shadows.
• No excessive rounded containers.
• No unnecessary borders.
• Keep the layout minimal and sophisticated.
• Every major visual section should respect the global 900px maximum height on desktop where applicable.
• The article content itself can naturally extend vertically because it is a reading experience.
• Prioritize typography, photography and readability.

01. ARTICLE HERO

Create a strong editorial hero.

Use a large, full-width featured image related to the article.

The image should occupy most of the viewport width and approximately 60–70% of the available viewport height.

Do not split the hero into two columns.

The image should be immersive and visually dominant.

Place the article information below or subtly over the image:

CATEGORY

ARTICLE TITLE

Short introduction / summary

Publication date

Example:

DESIGN

“O conforto como parte do projeto.”

“Como tecnologia, ergonomia e desenho podem trabalhar juntos para transformar a experiência de um espaço.”

12.08.2026

The title should be large and elegant, but not oversized.

Keep the composition clean.

02. ARTICLE HEADER INFORMATION

Below the hero image, create a restrained metadata area.

Display:

Category
Publication date
Reading time

Example:

DESIGN
12.08.2026
6 MIN READ

Do not use badges or pills.

03. ARTICLE CONTENT

Create a centered editorial reading column.

The reading width should be approximately 680–760px.

The body typography should be highly readable with comfortable line height and spacing.

Support:

• Paragraphs
• H2 headings
• H3 headings
• Bold text
• Italic text
• Quotes
• Lists
• Links
• Images
• Captions
• Videos
• Embedded media

Do not make the reading column too narrow.

Do not justify text.

Use strong typographic hierarchy.

04. EDITORIAL IMAGES

Images inside the article should not always be restricted to the text column.

Allow selected images to expand beyond the reading width when appropriate.

Use three possible image treatments:

FULL WIDTH
For important editorial images.

WIDE
For images that extend beyond the article column.

INLINE
For supporting images inside the article.

Every image should maintain its original aspect ratio.

Never crop important product or editorial imagery unnecessarily.

Add subtle captions when relevant.

05. PULL QUOTE

Allow important statements from the article to become large editorial pull quotes.

Example:

“Conforto não é apenas uma característica.
É parte da experiência de um espaço.”

The quote should create visual rhythm without becoming decorative.

06. ARTICLE NAVIGATION

At the end of the article, create a simple navigation area.

Previous article

← Artigo anterior

Next article

Próximo artigo →

Keep the navigation minimal.

Do not use large cards.

07. RELATED CONTENT

After the article navigation, create a related content section.

Eyebrow:

“CONTINUE LENDO”

Headline:

“Mais para descobrir.”

Display 3 related articles.

Use a clean editorial grid.

Each item should contain:

Image
Category
Title
Date
“Ler artigo ↗”

Do not use conventional rounded cards.

The related content should visually connect with the main Journal page.

08. FINAL CTA

End the article with a subtle Tempus institutional CTA.

Headline:

“Continue por perto.”

Supporting text:

“Explore mais conteúdos sobre design, arquitetura, tecnologia e novas formas de viver.”

CTA:

“Voltar para conteúdos ↗”

09. SOCIAL SHARING

Include discreet article sharing functionality.

Possible actions:

WhatsApp
LinkedIn
Copy link

Do not use large social media buttons.

They should remain secondary to the reading experience.

10. RESPONSIVE

Desktop:
Use a wide editorial hero and centered reading column.

Tablet:
Reduce hero height and content width while preserving hierarchy.

Mobile:
Use a full-width hero image.
Keep article title highly readable.
Use a comfortable reading width with appropriate side padding.
Stack all metadata vertically.
Keep related articles in a single column.

IMPORTANT CONTENT MANAGEMENT

The article page must be dynamic.

Each article should support:

title
slug
category
featured_image
summary
author
publication_date
reading_time
content
gallery
video
related_articles
seo_title
seo_description

The layout must automatically adapt to different article lengths and content types.

FINAL DIRECTION

The article page should feel like a Tempus editorial publication.

Think:

Architecture magazine
Design journal
Premium furniture editorial
Contemporary lifestyle publication

Not:

Corporate blog
News portal
WordPress template
SaaS interface

The content is the protagonist.

Typography and photography should create the experience.
