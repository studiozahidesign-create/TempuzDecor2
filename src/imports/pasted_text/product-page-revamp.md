The product detail page has a strong visual foundation, but it still feels like a sequence of independent sections instead of one continuous editorial experience.

The goal is NOT to redesign the visual identity.

The goal is to improve hierarchy, storytelling, pacing and section composition so the page feels comparable to a premium furniture brand rather than a traditional ecommerce product page.

Every section should naturally lead into the next.

--------------------------------------------------
GENERAL LAYOUT PRINCIPLE
--------------------------------------------------

The page should follow an editorial reading experience.

Avoid designing each section as an isolated block.

Avoid oversized section heights.

Avoid unnecessary empty vertical space.

Each section should exist because it contributes to the product story.

Whitespace should feel intentional, never accidental.

--------------------------------------------------
NEW CONTENT HIERARCHY
--------------------------------------------------

Reorganize the page into the following narrative sequence.

--------------------------------------------------
1. PRODUCT GALLERY
--------------------------------------------------

The page should begin with a large immersive gallery.

This remains the emotional entry point.

Maintain:

• large photography
• clean interface
• discreet thumbnails
• minimal controls

The gallery should immediately communicate craftsmanship.

--------------------------------------------------
2. PRODUCT INTRODUCTION
--------------------------------------------------

Immediately below the gallery:

Left column:

• Product name
• Collection
• Short editorial introduction
• CTA if needed

Right column:

Technical highlights only.

Do not overload this section.

Only display the most important specifications.

The objective is to introduce the product—not explain everything.

--------------------------------------------------
3. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------

Create a dedicated section for complete specifications.

Include:

• dimensions

• materials

• finishes

• mechanisms

• warranty

• available options

Keep this information organized and easy to scan.

Do not mix technical information with conceptual storytelling.

--------------------------------------------------
4. SIGNATURE
--------------------------------------------------

Reserve space for a future "Signature" section.

This section will become an important storytelling component.

It should introduce the philosophy behind the product, designer, craftsmanship or unique identity.

Prepare the layout so this block integrates naturally into the page.

Do not use placeholder content.

Only create the structure.

--------------------------------------------------
5. CONCEPTUAL PRODUCT STORY
--------------------------------------------------

Move the conceptual narrative after the technical information.

This section should explain:

why this product exists

its design philosophy

its emotional value

its relationship with architecture and everyday living

This is editorial content.

It should not read like marketing copy or technical documentation.

--------------------------------------------------
6. HOW TO EXPERIENCE THE PRODUCT
--------------------------------------------------

Create a dedicated "Usage" section.

Instead of explaining mechanisms only, explain how the product should be lived.

Examples:

comfort

reading

conversation

relaxation

daily rituals

ergonomics

long-term use

This section should feel human and aspirational.

--------------------------------------------------
7. MATERIALS & DETAILS
--------------------------------------------------

Maintain the close-up photography.

This is one of the strongest parts of the page.

Reduce unnecessary vertical spacing.

Increase the visual relationship between photography and descriptive text.

Allow images to dominate.

--------------------------------------------------
8. DIMENSIONS
--------------------------------------------------

Keep the dimensions section.

Simplify the presentation.

Allow more breathing room around the technical drawing.

Reduce visual noise.

Make measurements easier to scan.

--------------------------------------------------
9. COMPLETE TECHNICAL TABLE
--------------------------------------------------

Maintain the complete specification table.

Improve typography rhythm.

Increase alignment consistency.

Reduce oversized spacing between rows.

The table should feel elegant and architectural.

--------------------------------------------------
10. LIFESTYLE GALLERY
--------------------------------------------------

Maintain the architectural lifestyle photography.

This section is essential.

It reconnects the technical information with emotion.

Use large immersive photography.

Allow images to breathe.

--------------------------------------------------
11. RECOMMENDED PRODUCTS
--------------------------------------------------

Move the recommended products section below the lifestyle gallery.

The browsing experience should continue naturally.

Keep:

• product image

• name

• collection

• CTA

Improve:

• image quality

• spacing

• alignment

• hover interaction

--------------------------------------------------
12. FAQ
--------------------------------------------------

Maintain the FAQ.

Reduce its vertical height.

Use compact accordion spacing.

Avoid oversized empty containers.

--------------------------------------------------
13. STORE LOCATOR
--------------------------------------------------

Maintain the store locator as the final CTA before the footer.

This is the logical closing action.

--------------------------------------------------
SPACING
--------------------------------------------------

Review the entire page spacing.

Reduce excessive:

• top padding

• bottom padding

• section gaps

• empty containers

Avoid fixed viewport heights.

Sections should size themselves according to content.

--------------------------------------------------
DESIGN LANGUAGE
--------------------------------------------------

Every section should reinforce:

architecture

materiality

craftsmanship

comfort

calm

timelessness

premium quality

editorial storytelling

The page should feel like reading a beautifully designed furniture catalogue rather than scrolling through a conventional ecommerce product page.

--------------------------------------------------
FINAL EXPERIENCE
--------------------------------------------------

The reading flow should become:

Hero Gallery

↓

Product Introduction

↓

Technical Specifications

↓

Signature

↓

Concept Story

↓

How to Experience the Product

↓

Materials & Details

↓

Dimensions

↓

Complete Specifications

↓

Lifestyle Photography

↓

Recommended Products

↓

FAQ

↓

Store Locator

↓

Footer

The entire page should feel continuous, elegant and intentional, with each section naturally leading into the next while preserving the established Tempus visual identity.
